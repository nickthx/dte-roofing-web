# Plan 01-02: Competitor GBP Services & Categories Research

**Phase:** 1 — Diagnosis & Competitor Research
**Milestone:** v1.1
**Requirements:** HEAD-04
**Estimated tasks:** 4
**Depends on:** Nothing (can run in parallel with 01-01)

## Goal

Gather real Google Business Profile (GBP) Services and Categories data from the top 3 roofing competitors in each of 5 representative Central Ohio cities. This data informs all H2 content in Phases 2 and 4 so DTE Roofing's secondary service listings match what actual customers see in local search results.

## Context

- User asked for "gmbeverywhere" data — that's a Chrome extension for scraping GBP details. Not available as a tool.
- **Substitute:** `claude-in-chrome` MCP to navigate Google Maps, open competitor GBP profiles, and extract the Services + Categories fields directly from the page DOM.
- Fallback if bot detection trips: scrape competitor websites for their service page structure and infer categories from page titles / nav.

## Scope — 5 Representative Cities

Picked for geographic spread across the 13 service areas (north/south/east/west/central):

1. **Columbus** — central, largest market
2. **Hilliard** — west, HQ city
3. **Dublin** — northwest, affluent
4. **Westerville** — northeast
5. **Delaware** — far north (edge of service area)

For each city: top 3 competitors by GBP ranking for "roofer <city> OH".

## Tasks

### Task 1: Search & rank competitors per city
**What:** Use `claude-in-chrome` to navigate Google Maps for each of 5 cities, search `roofer <city> OH`, and record the top 3 results (excluding DTE Roofing itself if it appears).

**Steps per city:**
1. `mcp__claude-in-chrome__tabs_create_mcp` (new tab)
2. `mcp__claude-in-chrome__navigate` → `https://www.google.com/maps/search/roofer+<city>+OH`
3. `mcp__claude-in-chrome__get_page_text` to extract result list
4. Record top 3 business names, GBP URLs, star ratings, review counts

**Output:** Table in `research/v1.1-gbp-competitors.md`:
```
| City | Rank | Business | GBP URL | Rating | Reviews |
```

**Acceptance:** 15 rows (5 cities × 3 competitors).

### Task 2: Extract Services + Categories per competitor
**What:** For each of the 15 competitors, navigate to their GBP and extract:
- **Primary category** (e.g., "Roofing contractor")
- **Secondary categories** (e.g., "Gutter installer", "Siding contractor")
- **Services list** (the bulleted services shown in their GBP "Services" tab)

**Steps per competitor:**
1. Navigate to their GBP URL from Task 1
2. `get_page_text` on the main panel
3. Look for "Services" section and "Category" field
4. Record structured data

**Bot detection fallback:** If Google blocks scraping (captcha, rate limit), fall back to:
- Visit the competitor's own website (from their GBP's "Website" link)
- Read their navigation / services page
- Record inferred services from page structure

**Output:** Per-competitor block in `v1.1-gbp-competitors.md`:
```
### Columbus — #1 [Business Name]
- Primary: Roofing contractor
- Secondary: Gutter installer, Siding contractor
- Services: Roof repair, Roof replacement, Storm damage repair, Gutter installation, Metal roofing, ...
- Source: GBP direct | website fallback
```

**Acceptance:** 15 blocks, each with at least Primary category + 3 services listed.

### Task 3: Aggregate common services per city
**What:** For each of the 5 cities, compute the intersection + union of services across the top 3 competitors. Identify:
- **Table-stakes services** — listed by all 3 competitors (DTE should have H2 for these)
- **Differentiator services** — listed by 1-2 competitors (DTE might or might not)
- **City-specific signals** — anything notable (e.g., Delaware competitors emphasize "rural roofing" or "metal buildings")

**Output:** Aggregation section in `v1.1-gbp-competitors.md`:
```
## Columbus — Service Aggregate
**Table stakes (3/3 competitors):** Roof repair, Roof replacement, Gutter installation, Storm damage
**Common (2/3):** Metal roofing, Commercial roofing
**Unique signals:** ...
```

**Acceptance:** 5 city aggregate blocks.

### Task 4: Derive H2 recommendations per DTE page
**What:** Produce a mapping from competitor data → recommended H2 content for DTE's location + service pages.

**Output:** Final section in `v1.1-gbp-competitors.md`:
```
## H2 Recommendations for v1.1 Phases 2 & 4

### /locations/columbus
Primary H1: "Roof Repair & Replacement in Columbus, OH"
Recommended H2s (driven by competitor table stakes):
- "Columbus Roofing Services: Repairs, Replacements, and Storm Damage"
- "Gutter Installation & Repair in Columbus"
- "Metal & Commercial Roofing"

### /services/roof-repair
Primary H1: "Roof Repair in Columbus, OH"
Recommended H2s:
- "Common Roof Repairs We Handle in Central Ohio"
- "Storm Damage & Emergency Repairs"
- ...
```

**Acceptance:**
- Recommendations cover all 13 location pages (extrapolate from the 5 researched cities for the 8 unresearched ones based on geographic proximity)
- Recommendations cover all 12 service pages
- Each recommendation cites which competitor(s) inspired it

## Risks & Fallbacks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Google Maps triggers captcha | Use website fallback per competitor (documented in Task 2) |
| GBP Services tab hidden behind a click | Use `mcp__claude-in-chrome__form_input` or `computer` to click "More" before extracting |
| 5 cities insufficient | Extrapolate to 13 cities in Task 4 using nearest researched city; explicitly flag extrapolations |
| Competitors don't have GBP Services populated | Fall back to their website navigation; note data quality per competitor |
