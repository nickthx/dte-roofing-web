## Methodology & Data-Source Note

**Intended method (plan 01-02):** `claude-in-chrome` MCP → Google Maps Local Pack → scrape GBP Services + Categories per competitor.

**Actual method (fallback):** The `claude-in-chrome` MCP was not available in the execution environment. Google Maps Local Pack results require client-side JavaScript and were not retrievable via `curl`. The plan explicitly anticipates this situation:

> **Bot detection fallback:** If Google blocks scraping, fall back to: visit the competitor's own website (from their GBP "Website" link), read their navigation/services page, record inferred services from page structure.

Every competitor below was researched by:
1. Fetching their homepage (and where available, their services page) via `curl` using a standard Chrome user-agent
2. Stripping scripts/styles/tags to plain text
3. Regex-matching against a 41-pattern roofing service dictionary (roof repair, roof replacement, gutter installation, storm damage, metal roofing, etc.)
4. Extracting `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and city mentions to validate Central Ohio service area

**Data source column** in every competitor block documents whether the source was homepage scrape, services page scrape, or both. No GBP direct data was captured — this is acknowledged as a quality limitation. For the downstream goal (informing H2 content), website service listings are an acceptable proxy because most roofers mirror their GBP services on their own website.

**Ranking caveat:** Without Google Maps access, I cannot rank competitors #1/#2/#3 by actual Local Pack position. Instead, the 15 rows below are competitor–city assignments based on:
- Whether the competitor's homepage explicitly mentions that city in its service area
- The competitor's known market presence and service richness
- Geographic fit (e.g., Delaware is edge-of-service-area and typically served by Columbus-based roofers)

Ranks 1/2/3 within each city are assigned by **service richness** (total service count) and **city-mention strength**, not by verified GBP position. Each row is flagged with confidence: **strong** (explicit city mention + ≥10 services), **medium** (inferred from geographic/market presence + rich services), **low** (inferred only).

## Summary of Cross-Cutting H2 Themes

Across all 13 locations and 12 services, the following H2 patterns recur most often because they are **table stakes in every researched city**:

1. **"{City} Roof Repair, Replacement & Installation"** — 3/3 in every city
2. **"Gutter Installation, Repair & Guards"** — 3/3 or 2/3 in every city
3. **"Storm Damage, Hail Damage & Insurance Claims"** — 3/3 or 2/3 in every city (Central Ohio is a storm belt)
4. **"Commercial Roofing"** — 3/3 in Columbus/Dublin/Westerville; 2/3 in Hilliard/Delaware
5. **"Metal Roofing & Asphalt Shingle Options"** — 3/3 in most cities
6. **"Roof Inspection & Maintenance"** — 2/3 across cities

**DTE differentiator opportunities** (where most competitors do NOT emphasize):
- **Preventative Maintenance Plans** — no competitor uses "preventative" as a distinct service keyword
- **Drone / Attic Roof Inspection methods** — Muth and Summit imply but do not H2 explicitly
- **Delaware-first positioning** — Delaware is treated as "northern edge" by every competitor
- **Hilliard hometown positioning** — no competitor claims Hilliard as HQ
- **Education-driven H2s** ("When to Repair vs. Replace", "Signs You Need a New Roof") — absent from competitor H2s

## Known Limitations

1. **No GBP direct data.** The claude-in-chrome MCP was not available. All data comes from competitor website scrapes, not Google Business Profile Services/Categories tabs. If a competitor's GBP has services their website doesn't mention, those are missed. The plan explicitly anticipates this fallback in its "Risks & Fallbacks" section.

2. **No verified Local Pack rankings.** Ranks 1/2/3 within each city are assigned by service richness + city-mention strength, not by actual Google Maps Local Pack position. If a later browser-enabled session is possible, the pool of 8 competitors can be re-ordered.

3. **Some cities share the same competitor set.** Because 5 regional firms (Muth, Bone Dry, Summit, 614 Exteriors, Lifetime Quality) genuinely dominate most of Central Ohio, the same competitors appear across multiple cities. This reflects market reality. If Phase 2 / 4 need more per-city variety, local independent roofers (likely smaller GBP footprint) can be added in a follow-up research pass.

4. **8 extrapolated cities.** Powell, Worthington, Upper Arlington, Gahanna, New Albany, Grove City, Reynoldsburg, Pickerington — H2 recommendations are derived from nearest-researched-city + market presence signals, not direct research. Phase 2/4 editors should treat these as starting drafts.

5. **4 competitors explicitly DID NOT mention target cities on homepage** but are included for market-presence reasons:
   - Shamrock Roofing (only "columbus" on homepage — regional-specific pages elsewhere)
   - Columbus Exteriors (columbus only — thin service catalog)
   - Columbus Roofing Pros (columbus + upper arlington only)
   - Lifetime Quality Roofing (multi-state — selective city mention)
