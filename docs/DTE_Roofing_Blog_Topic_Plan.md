**Audience:** Central Ohio homeowners (Columbus metro + 13 service-area suburbs) researching roof problems, costs, and contractors — primarily before they've chosen a roofer.

**Strategic note:** Your *service pages* (`/services/roof-repair`, `/services/roof-replacement`, etc.) already target the commercial/transactional keywords ("roof replacement Columbus"). This blog cluster deliberately targets **informational** keywords ("how do I know if I need a new roof", "what does hail damage look like") so it does NOT cannibalize your service pages — instead each post funnels readers *toward* the relevant service page. This is also exactly the kind of content ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI cite when a Columbus homeowner asks a roofing question, which builds your AI visibility.

## Topic Details

### 1. Roof Repair vs. Replacement: A Columbus Guide  *(PILLAR)*
- **Keyword:** roof repair vs replacement
- **Intent:** Commercial · **Funnel:** MOFU · **Type:** Pillar guide · **Difficulty:** Medium
- **Why:** This is *the* question every homeowner with a roof problem asks before spending money. Owning it positions you as the honest advisor — which is your whole brand.
- **Angle:** Lean hard into your "we only recommend what's actually needed" reputation. Give an honest decision framework (age, damage extent, cost ratio) instead of pushing replacement. This is the trust differentiator most roofer blogs won't write.
- **Key sections:** When a repair is enough · Warning signs that point to replacement · The 30%-of-replacement-cost rule of thumb · Repair vs. replace cost ranges in Central Ohio · How DTE's free inspection settles the question.

### 2. 7 Signs You Need a New Roof in Ohio
- **Keyword:** signs you need a new roof · **Intent:** Informational · **Funnel:** TOFU · **Type:** Listicle · **Difficulty:** Low
- **Why:** High-volume, low-difficulty, and the perfect top-of-funnel entry point — homeowners search this before they even know they need a roofer.
- **Angle:** Make it Ohio-specific (granule loss after hail, curling from freeze-thaw, ice-dam staining) rather than generic. Add a "what's normal vs. what's urgent" note for each sign.
- **Key sections:** Each sign with a photo + plain-English explanation · Which signs are urgent vs. monitor · When to book a free inspection.

### 3. What Hail Damage Looks Like on a Roof
- **Keyword:** hail damage roof · **Intent:** Informational · **Funnel:** TOFU/MOFU · **Type:** Guide · **Difficulty:** Low
- **Why:** Central Ohio gets regular hail; homeowners search this right after a storm, primed to act. Timely now (spring/summer storm season).
- **Angle:** Show real close-up examples (use your storm-damage job photos), explain the difference between cosmetic and functional damage, and note that hail damage often isn't visible from the ground — which is why a free inspection matters.
- **Key sections:** What hail bruising/granule loss looks like · Cosmetic vs. functional damage · Why you can't always see it from the ground · The insurance-claim window. **Links to → /services/storm-damage.**
